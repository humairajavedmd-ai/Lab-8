# Defect Log - PSVS-REQ-v1.0

| Defect ID | Req ID | Category | Description | Evidence | Severity | Suggested fix | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DEF-01 | S1 | Incompleteness | 'Registered' is never defined: who registers depositors, what is checked, and how registration is revoked or suspended. | S1: "only from registered depositors" - no registration process, registry or status lifecycle exists in S1-S12. | High | Add a requirement defining the depositor registry (registration, verification, suspension, revocation) and state that deposits from non-registered or suspended parties are rejected and logged. | Open |
| DEF-02 | S1 | Ambiguity | 'Accept' is unclear: does it mean physical receipt, entry into the inventory, or placement in a chamber? Checks on the box (seal, species, sample count) are not stated. | S1 uses 'accept' only; S11 uses 'redeposited' and S3 uses 'hold' for the same lifecycle. | Medium | Define the deposit lifecycle states (received, inspected, accepted, stored) and say which state 'accept' refers to. | Open |
| DEF-03 | S1, S4 | Ambiguity | Terminology is inconsistent: scenario says 'institutions', S1 and S4 say 'depositor', S5 says 'requesting party'. It is unclear whether these are the same actor type. | Scenario: 'registered institutions'; S1/S4 'depositor'; S5 'any requesting party'. | Low | Add a glossary and use one term per concept (Depositor, Vault Authority, Staff, Requester). | Open |
| DEF-04 | S2 | Vagueness | 'A large number of boxes' has no quantity, so S2 cannot be designed against or tested. | S2: "can hold a large number of boxes" - 'large' is unquantified. | Medium | Delete S2 or replace it with the quantified limits in S3 plus an overall system capacity. | Open |
| DEF-05 | S2, S3 | Redundancy | S2 is subsumed by S3, which gives the actual capacity numbers; S2 adds no testable content. | S2 (large capacity) vs S3 (50,000 / 20,000 limits). | Low | Merge S2 into S3 and keep one capacity requirement. | Open |
| DEF-06 | S3 | Incompleteness | Only chambers 1-4 are specified while the scenario says 'several' chambers. It is not stated whether more chambers may exist, and nothing says what happens when a chamber is full or how a chamber is chosen for a new box. | S3: "Chambers 1-3 ... Chamber 4"; no full-chamber behaviour or allocation rule. | High | State the exact chamber set (or how to add chambers), total capacity (170,000), the allocation rule, and the behaviour at capacity (reject the deposit and log it). | Open |
| DEF-07 | S3 | Ambiguity | Capacity is counted in 'boxes' but box size/volume is never defined. It is unclear whether withdrawn, quarantined or reserved boxes count toward the limit. | S3: "at most 50,000 boxes" - no box dimension or counting rule. | Low | Define a standard box unit and state which box states occupy a slot. | Open |
| DEF-08 | S4 | Incompleteness | Nothing covers a depositor that is merged, dissolved, deregistered, or that wants to delegate withdrawal to an agent. 'Only that depositor' leaves boxes stuck with no valid withdrawer. | S4: "only that depositor may withdraw it" - no succession or delegation rule. | Medium | Add rules for successor depositors, delegated agents and orphaned boxes. | Open |
| DEF-09 | S4, S5 | Contradiction | S4 says ONLY the owning depositor may withdraw a box; S5 says the vault authority may release ANY box to ANY requesting party. Both cannot hold at the same time. | S4: "only that depositor may withdraw" vs S5: "may release any box to any requesting party". | Critical | Make S5 an explicit, bounded exception to S4: S4 applies 'except as permitted by S5', S5 is limited to a defined emergency, and the owner is notified. | Open |
| DEF-10 | S5 | Vagueness | 'Emergency' is undefined: no criteria, no declaring authority, no start or end condition. | S5: "In an emergency" - undefined term. | High | Define emergency types (e.g., declared food-security crisis, chamber failure) and who declares and ends one. | Open |
| DEF-11 | S5 | Ambiguity | 'Vault authority' is undefined and 'any requesting party' is unbounded: it is unclear who the authority is, whether one person can act alone, and whether the requester must be identified or vetted. | S5: "the vault authority", "any requesting party". | High | Define the authority (role, quorum/dual approval) and the requester identification required. | Open |
| DEF-12 | S5 | Incompleteness | No audit, owner notification, record-keeping or return/compensation rule for emergency releases; it is unclear who owns the box afterwards. | S5 has no reference to S12 or to the owning depositor. | High | Require that every emergency release is logged, the owner is notified, and ownership/return status is recorded. | Open |
| DEF-13 | S5, S10 | Contradiction | It is unclear whether emergency releases count toward a depositor's 20% annual withdrawal limit. S10 reads as absolute, S5 as an unrestricted release. | S10: "at most 20% ... per year" vs S5: "any box". | Medium | State that S10 applies to depositor-initiated withdrawals only (or explicitly includes emergency releases). | Open |
| DEF-14 | S6 | Vagueness | 'Safe temperature' is not defined, so it is not testable. | S6: "stored at a safe temperature". | High | Replace with a numeric range, e.g., a nominal storage band and an absolute upper limit of -18 degrees C. | Open |
| DEF-15 | S6, S7 | Redundancy | S6 states the same intent as S7 in vague terms; S7 gives the actual limit. They overlap and could diverge in later changes. | S6 (safe temperature) vs S7 (never above -18 degrees C). | Low | Merge S6 into S7 and keep a single quantified temperature requirement. | Open |
| DEF-16 | S7 | Infeasibility | 'Never rise above -18 degrees C' is an absolute guarantee that cannot be met for power failure, door openings or sensor faults. | S7: "shall never rise above" - no tolerance, duration or fault condition. | High | Replace it with a bounded requirement: stay at or below -18 degrees C, an alarm within a stated time on an excursion, and a maximum excursion duration. | Open |
| DEF-17 | S7 | Incompleteness | Only an upper limit is given: no lower bound or nominal setpoint. Where the temperature is measured (air, wall, box core), how often, and the response to an excursion are not stated. | S7 gives a single upper bound. | Medium | Add a nominal range, measurement point and interval, and the alarm/recovery behaviour. | Open |
| DEF-18 | S7, S8 | Contradiction | S7 forbids any chamber from exceeding -18 degrees C; S8 allows a chamber to be warmed to 0 degrees C, which is 18 degrees higher. | S7: "never ... above -18C" vs S8: "may be warmed to 0C". | Critical | Scope S7 as 'except during an authorised maintenance window per S8', and require boxes to be relocated or handled to protect them during the window. | Open |
| DEF-19 | S8 | Incompleteness | Maintenance conditions are missing: who authorises it, how long, what happens to the boxes inside, and how the chamber is returned to -18 degrees C before reuse. | S8: "a chamber may be warmed to 0C" - no procedure. | High | Define the maintenance procedure: authorisation, empty or relocate boxes first, maximum duration, and recovery check. | Open |
| DEF-20 | S8 | Ambiguity | 'Maintenance' is undefined, and '0 degrees C' could be a target or a maximum; 'may' makes it optional and unconditional. | S8: "During maintenance ... warmed to 0C". | Low | Define the maintenance types and state that 0 degrees C is a maximum. | Open |
| DEF-21 | S3, S8 | Infeasibility | If a chamber is emptied for maintenance, its boxes must fit elsewhere, but chambers may already be near capacity under S3. The relocation cannot be guaranteed. | S3 capacity limits vs S8 maintenance warming. | Medium | Add a relocation rule and a reserve-capacity policy. | Open |
| DEF-22 | S9 | Vagueness | 'Low viability' has no threshold or measure (e.g., germination percentage). | S9: "low viability" - no number. | High | Set a species-specific viability threshold (e.g., germination below X %) and the test method. | Open |
| DEF-23 | S9 | Non-verifiability | 'Promptly' has no time bound, so it can never be demonstrated by a test. | S9: "flagged promptly". | Medium | Specify a maximum time, e.g., flagged within 24 hours of the test result. | Open |
| DEF-24 | S9 | Incompleteness | The requirement does not say who measures viability, how often samples are tested, who is notified, or how regeneration is carried out. | S9 has no actor, test interval or notification. | High | Add test frequency, the responsible role, the owner notification and a regeneration workflow. | Open |
| DEF-25 | S4, S9 | Contradiction | Regeneration usually requires taking seeds out of the box, but S4 allows only the owning depositor to withdraw. Staff cannot act on a flagged box without the depositor. | S9 (flag for regeneration) vs S4 (depositor-only withdrawal). | Medium | Define regeneration as a depositor-authorised action, or an explicit exception for staff-run regeneration with the owner's consent. | Open |
| DEF-26 | S10 | Ambiguity | '20% of its boxes of a species per year' is ambiguous: calendar vs rolling year, 20% of the holdings at the start of the year or of current holdings, rounding of fractions, and handling of boxes deposited during the year. | S10: "at most 20% of its boxes of a species per year". | High | Define the year, the baseline count (e.g., holdings at 1 January) and the rounding rule (round down). | Open |
| DEF-27 | S10, S11 | Incompleteness | It is not stated whether a redeposited box restores the depositor's quota or counts as new stock, so the quota can be gamed or blocked. | S10 (annual limit) and S11 (redeposit) do not refer to each other. | Medium | State how withdrawals and redeposits affect the quota counter. | Open |
| DEF-28 | S10, Scenario | Incompleteness | S10 depends on 'species' and on each box holding one species, but this appears only in the scenario text, not in S1-S12; how species are identified is undefined. | Scenario: "Each box holds seed samples of one species"; S10 uses 'species'. | Medium | Add a requirement that every box is tagged with one species identifier from a controlled list. | Open |
| DEF-29 | S11 | Ambiguity | 'Re-inspection' is not defined: who does it, what is checked, what the pass criteria are, and what happens to a box that fails. | S11: "only after re-inspection". | High | Define the inspection checklist, inspector role, and the outcomes (accept, quarantine, reject). | Open |
| DEF-30 | S11 | Untraceability | S11 has no link to S9 (viability) or S1 (who may redeposit), and there is no rationale or source for it, so tests and design items cannot be traced back to it. | S1-S12 have no source, rationale or priority fields. | Low | Add IDs with rationale, source, priority and links between related requirements. | Open |
| DEF-31 | S12 | Vagueness | 'Good audit trail of all actions' is subjective: 'good' and 'all actions' are undefined. | S12: "keep a good audit trail of all actions". | High | List the audited events and fields (who, what, when, which box, outcome), and replace 'good' with measurable properties. | Open |
| DEF-32 | S12 | Non-verifiability | Nothing says how long records are kept, who can read them, or that they must be tamper-evident, so compliance cannot be checked. | S12 gives no retention, access or integrity criteria. | Medium | Add retention period, access control and tamper-evidence requirements. | Open |
| DEF-33 | S1-S12 | Incompleteness | Staff roles (deposits, withdrawals, monitoring) appear in the scenario but no requirement defines them or their permissions; there is no requirement for withdrawal or inspection workflows beyond S4/S10/S11. | Scenario: "staff handle deposits, withdrawals, and monitoring". | Medium | Add role and permission requirements for staff and the vault authority. | Open |
